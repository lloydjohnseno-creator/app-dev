# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

> "Friends don't lie." — Eleven

 About the Series
Stranger Things is a *science fiction horror* series set in the 1980s in the small town of Hawkins, Indiana. It follows a group of kids who uncover secret experiments and a dangerous parallel dimension called the **Upside Down**.

---

Main Characters
1. Eleven
2. Mike Wheeler
3. Dustin Henderson
4. Steve Harrington

Why I Like It
- Great mix of mystery and adventure
- Strong friendships between characters
- Nostalgic 1980s music and style
